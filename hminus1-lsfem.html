Do We Really Need an H−1-Norm LSFEM?

我們真的需要 H-1-範數 LSFEM 嗎？

No. A divergence splitting of the data restores a standard L2-residual first-order system.

不需要。對數據作散度分解，就可以恢復一個標準的 L2-殘差一階系統。

The Conventional View and the Divergence Splitting 傳統觀點與散度分解

First-order system least-squares finite element methods are usually understood as methods on the L2-scale: the first-order equations are measured by L2 residuals. From this viewpoint, an H−1 right-hand side appears to require an H−1-norm least-squares formulation, as in the negative-norm least-squares approach associated with Bramble and related work.

一階系統最小二乘有限元方法通常被理解為 L2-scale 上的方法：一階方程的殘差都在 L2 中度量。按照這個思路，若右端項只屬於 H-1，似乎就必須把 least-squares residual 改到 H-1 norm 中，這正是 Bramble 等人的 negative-norm least-squares 路線。

That change of norm is not necessary. The standard L2-based LSFEM can be retained if the data are split correctly.不需要改變殘差範數。只要正確分解數據，就可以完整保留標準的 L2-based LSFEM。

Write the right-hand side in divergence-split form

F=f1−∇⋅f2,f1∈L2,f2∈L2(Ω)d.

The divergence part is then absorbed into the flux variable. After this change of variable, both first-order equations again have L2 residuals. Thus the difficulty is resolved by a structural reformulation, not by replacing the least-squares norm.

把右端項寫成 divergence splitting，然後把散度部分吸收到通量變量中。經過這個變量重定義後，兩個一階方程重新都具有 L2 殘差。因此，問題是通過結構性的重新表述解決的，而不是通過更換 least-squares norm 解決的。

The key idea is not an H−1-norm LSFEM, but an H−1-data splitting that restores an L2-residual first-order system.關鍵不是構造 H-1-norm LSFEM，而是對 H-1 數據作分解，從而恢復一個具有 L2 殘差的一階系統。

The Mixed-Boundary Problem 混合邊界值問題

Consider a general second-order elliptic problem whose right-hand side is represented by an L2L^2 scalar part and the distributional divergence of an L2L^2 vector field:

−∇⋅(A∇u)+b⋅∇u+cu=f1−∇⋅f2in Ω, -\nabla\!\cdot(A\nabla u)+b\cdot\nabla u+cu = f_1-\nabla\!\cdot f_2 \quad\text{in }\Omega,
u=ϕon ΓD,(−A∇u−f2)⋅n=ψon ΓN. u=\phi \quad\text{on }\Gamma_D, \qquad (-A\nabla u-f_2)\cdot n=\psi \quad\text{on }\Gamma_N.

考慮一般二階橢圓問題。右端項由一個 L2L^2 純量部分與一個 L2L^2 向量場的分佈散度組成，並配以 Dirichlet-Neumann 混合邊界條件。

With f1∈L2(Ω)f_1\in L^2(\Omega) and f2∈L2(Ω)df_2\in L^2(\Omega)^d, the full right-hand side belongs naturally to H−1H^{-1}. The divergence of f2f_2 need not be an L2L^2 function.

Why the Gradient Flux May Fail 為什麼梯度通量可能失效

The usual gradient flux −A∇u-A\nabla u belongs to L2(Ω)dL^2(\Omega)^d, but its divergence may exist only in H−1(Ω)H^{-1}(\Omega). Hence, in general,

−A∇u∉H(div⁡;Ω). -A\nabla u \notin H(\operatorname{div};\Omega).
An L2L^2 vector field is not automatically an H(div⁡)H(\operatorname{div}) flux. 一個 L2L^2 向量場不會自動成為 H(div⁡)H(\operatorname{div}) 通量。

這不是證明中的小技術問題。如果通量不屬於 H(div⁡)H(\operatorname{div})，以其散度作為 L2L^2 殘差的標準一階最小二乘格式便沒有建立在正確的函數空間上。

Absorb the Data into the Flux 把數據吸收到通量中

The correct first-order variable is the shifted flux

σ:=−A∇u−f2. \sigma := -A\nabla u-f_2.

Then the distributional part of the data disappears from the balance residual:

σ+A∇u+f2=0,∇⋅σ+b⋅∇u+cu=f1. \sigma+A\nabla u+f_2=0, \qquad \nabla\!\cdot\sigma+b\cdot\nabla u+cu=f_1.

Because the second right-hand side is now in L2L^2, the shifted variable satisfies σ∈H(div⁡;Ω)\sigma\in H(\operatorname{div};\Omega).

The H−1H^{-1} part of the data should be absorbed into the flux, not left inside an L2L^2 divergence residual. 數據中的 H−1H^{-1} 部分應被吸收到通量中，而不應留在一個要求屬於 L2L^2 的散度殘差內。

Boundary Conditions for the Shifted Flux 修正通量的邊界條件

Once the data are incorporated into the flux, the Neumann condition must be imposed on that same shifted flux:

σ⋅n=ψon ΓN. \sigma\cdot n=\psi \quad\text{on }\Gamma_N.

The natural affine spaces are therefore

X:={v∈H1(Ω):v=ϕ on ΓD}, X:=\{v\in H^1(\Omega):v=\phi\text{ on }\Gamma_D\},
Σp:={τ∈H(div⁡;Ω):τ⋅n=ψ on ΓN}. \Sigma:=\{\tau\in H(\operatorname{div};\Omega): \tau\cdot n=\psi\text{ on }\Gamma_N\}.

一旦 f2f_2 被吸收到通量定義中，Neumann 數據也必須施加在同一個修正通量的正常跡上。勢變量與通量變量分別位於帶給定 Dirichlet 跡和 Neumann 正常跡的仿射空間中。

The shifted flux definition and its normal boundary condition are one structure; they cannot be chosen independently. 修正通量的定義與其法向邊界條件屬於同一個結構，不能彼此獨立選取。

The Equivalent First-Order System 等價的一階系統

σ+A∇u+f2=0in Ω,∇⋅σ+b⋅∇u+cu=f1in Ω,u=ϕon ΓD,σ⋅n=ψon ΓN, \boxed{\begin{aligned} \sigma+A\nabla u+f_2&=0 &&\text{in }\Omega,\\ \nabla\!\cdot\sigma+b\cdot\nabla u+cu&=f_1 &&\text{in }\Omega,\\ u&=\phi &&\text{on }\Gamma_D,\\ \sigma\cdot n&=\psi &&\text{on }\Gamma_N, \end{aligned}}

Thus (σ,u)∈Σp×X(\sigma,u)\in\Sigma\times X. The weak second-order problem and this first-order system are equivalent, with the normal trace identified through Green's formula.

因此 (σ,u)∈Σp×X(\sigma,u)\in\Sigma\times X。弱形式的二階問題與這個一階系統等價，而修正通量的正常跡由 Green 公式識別。

The Least-Squares Functional 最小二乘泛函

The two equations now have genuine L2L^2 residuals. A natural least-squares functional is

ℒ(τ,v)=∥A−1/2(τ+A∇v+f2)∥02+∥∇⋅τ+b⋅∇v+cv−f1∥02, \mathcal L(\tau,v)= \|A^{-1/2}(\tau+A\nabla v+f_2)\|_0^2 +\|\nabla\!\cdot\tau+b\cdot\nabla v+cv-f_1\|_0^2,

minimized over Σp×X\Sigma\times X, or over the corresponding homogeneous spaces after taking boundary liftings.

選對通量後，兩個方程都具有真正的 L2L^2 殘差。邊界數據可以直接編碼在仿射試探空間中，也可以先作 lifting，再在相應的齊次空間上極小化。

The Structural Chain 完整的結構鏈

H−1 data⇒−A∇u may not be in H(div⁡)⇒σ=−A∇u−f2 H^{-1}\text{ data} \Longrightarrow -A\nabla u\text{ may not be in }H(\operatorname{div}) \Longrightarrow \sigma=-A\nabla u-f_2 ⇒σ⋅n=ψ on ΓN⇒a valid L2-residual LSFEM. \Longrightarrow \sigma\cdot n=\psi\text{ on }\Gamma_N \Longrightarrow \text{a valid }L^2\text{-residual LSFEM}.

這個構造保留了 conforming H(div⁡)×H1H(\operatorname{div})\times H^1 離散、對稱正定代數系統、準最佳逼近、內建最小二乘誤差估計，以及逐單元局部化與自適應加密。

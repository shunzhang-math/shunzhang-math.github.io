Research Interests 研究興趣

My research focuses on finite element methods, particularly least-squares and mixed formulations, adaptive algorithms, singular-source and interface problems, reduced-order modeling, and scientific machine learning.

我的研究聚焦於有限元方法，尤其是最小二乘與混合格式、自適應算法、奇異源與界面問題、降階建模，以及科學機器學習。
